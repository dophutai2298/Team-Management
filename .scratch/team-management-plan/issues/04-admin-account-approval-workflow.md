# 04: Admin account approval workflow

**What to build:** Give Admins an account approval workflow that turns pending verified users into active employees with official org data.

**Blocked by:** 03: Auth session, registration, and pending access gate.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Admin can list pending accounts with loading, empty, and error states.
- [ ] Admin can approve a pending account after verifying or editing the employee code.
- [ ] Admin can reject a pending account with a reason.
- [ ] Approval captures initial team, manager, role, position, and level.
- [ ] Approved users become Active and can access protected app features.
- [ ] Approval and rejection actions are audited.
