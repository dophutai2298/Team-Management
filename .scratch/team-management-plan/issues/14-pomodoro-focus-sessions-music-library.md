# 14: Pomodoro focus sessions and music library

**What to build:** Add a focus timer, break timer, focus session tracking, simple statistics, and Admin-managed music library.

**Blocked by:** 03: Auth session, registration, and pending access gate; 05: Role, permission catalog, and authorization matrix.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] Employees can run focus and break timers.
- [ ] Completed and interrupted focus sessions are recorded with duration, mode, timestamps, and optional task link.
- [ ] Employees can see today and weekly focus summaries.
- [ ] Admin can manage music library entries with enabled/disabled state.
- [ ] Music entries support name, description, background image, and YouTube URL.
- [ ] Focus session and music actions are authorized by owner/admin permissions.
